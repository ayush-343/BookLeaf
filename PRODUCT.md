# BookLeaf Author Support Portal

## Platform

A responsive web app for a personal hiring assessment.

## Users and jobs

Authors check their books and royalty totals, ask about a book or their account, and follow the support conversation. Staff triage incoming tickets, review policy and book facts, and send a reply.

## Product boundaries

Authors see only their records and public messages. Staff can manage all tickets and add private notes. AI suggests category, priority, and reply text. Staff control every send. The supplied fixture is the source for book facts and royalty totals. Unknown bank status, publishing timelines, and payout history stay unknown.

## Stack

Next.js App Router with a REST API, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Better Auth, TanStack Query, and OpenRouter. Vercel Hobby and Neon Free for the assessment demo.

## Visual direction

Use BookLeaf Publishing’s public brand: coral actions, blush accents, white surfaces, and Lato. The book list and ticket conversation are the main surfaces. The admin reply editor sits beside the supporting policy and book facts.
